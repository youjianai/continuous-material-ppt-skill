# QA rules

- Confirm the requested slide count and 16:9 canvas.
- Render every slide individually, then inspect a montage for rhythm and consistency.
- Check title wrapping, body readability, image cropping, contrast, and canvas bounds.
- Confirm there are no empty placeholders or accidental hard-edge overlays in continuous backgrounds.
- Confirm every slide has speaker notes and a `[Sources]` block when external material or non-trivial claims are present.
- Confirm evidence labels state what the artifact proves and what it does not prove.
- Open the final file in the target presentation app when possible. Report opening success separately from full visual playback validation.
