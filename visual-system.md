# Continuous-material visual system

- Canvas: 16:9. Use a full-bleed continuous field of fluid, reflective, white, silver, and light-gray material. Backgrounds must flow across the whole canvas.
- Palette: white, silver, graphite, cool blue, and occasional warm amber for decisions or risk.
- Typography: Microsoft YaHei or another stable Windows sans-serif. Keep titles large, body text readable, and hierarchy obvious.
- Composition: one dominant composition per slide. Avoid card grids, dashboards, hard left/right image splits, isolated 3D objects on white, and decorative clutter.
- Text: place it in low-information areas of the background. Keep titles to one line where possible; shorten copy before reducing type size.
- Evidence: use original screenshots, posters, or documents supplied by the user. Crop or blend them into the material field, with a clear source note.
- Rhythm: alternate quiet explanatory slides, evidence slides, process/architecture slides, and a clear decision close.
