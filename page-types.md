# Page types

Choose only the page types needed by the story:

- Cover: one thesis, one short supporting line.
- Tension: a concrete problem and why it matters.
- Evidence: one real artifact and the conclusion it supports.
- Comparison: two or three meaningful differences, not a feature inventory.
- Process: a short sequence with clear ownership or gates.
- Architecture: stable layers, boundaries, and human review points.
- Capabilities: three to five observable abilities tied to an outcome.
- Budget or gates: assumptions, amounts, release conditions, and stop rules.
- Action close: the decisions or next actions requested from the audience.

For dense material, split the slide or move detail to notes. Do not use tiny type to preserve a fixed slide count.
